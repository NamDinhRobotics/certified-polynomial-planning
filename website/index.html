01 / METHOD

Constructive recovery.
Exact reference checks.

A semidefinite relaxation provides a bound and a candidate. Recovery constructs a physical trajectory; exact predicates check the resulting reference.

A structured correction
to the polynomial reference.

γ̃(s) = γ(s) + α z(s)e

z(s) positive homogeneous modeα finite correction amplitudee physical direction

Under a sufficient endpoint-line condition, a positive homogeneous spline mode yields a collision-free correction. Exact Bernstein subdivision constructs a sufficient amplitude.

The optimization layer uses a value-preserving factor profile, guarded numerical computation, and conic fallback. Reference feasibility and objective bounds are checked separately.

The recovery condition is sufficient, not universal. Workspace, energy, runtime, and tracking guarantees require separate checks.

WHAT IS CERTIFIED?

Physical checks certify the represented rational reference against the specified constraints. Tracking success is measured from simulation telemetry. The exact lower–upper interval certifies the root SDP relaxation objective; it does not establish global optimality of the recovered physical trajectory or continuous closed-loop safety. Forty original numerical root rejections remain recorded.
